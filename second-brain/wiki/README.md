Claude's domain. Every page here is written by `/brain:ingest` from sources in
`raw/`, against the schema in `../CLAUDE.md`. Read these; don't hand-edit them,
because the next ingest is written against the schema rather than against your
edits. To change what the pages say, change the schema or the source.

- `log.md` — append-only activity log.
- `sources/` — one summary per source document.
- `concepts/` — principles and mechanisms.
- `projects/` — workstreams and milestones.
- `decisions/` — choices made and questions still open.
- `people/` — collaborator dossiers.
